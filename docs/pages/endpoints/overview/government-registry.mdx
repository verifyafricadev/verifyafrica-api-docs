# Government Registry Checks

Verify identity and registry records against official government databases across South Africa, Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya.

## Countries

- [🇿🇦 South Africa](./overview/government-registry/south-africa) — 1 endpoint
- [🇳🇬 Nigeria](./overview/government-registry/nigeria) — 7 endpoints
- [🇬🇭 Ghana](./overview/government-registry/ghana) — 4 endpoints
- [🇰🇪 Kenya](./overview/government-registry/kenya) — 4 endpoints

## Getting Started

Government registry checks use one product endpoint, organized by country in the [API Reference](/api):

`POST /api/v2/public/verifications/requests/government_registry_checks/`

Include `verification_type` in the body to select the specific check (for example `ng_bvn_verification`).

Browse a country below, then open the check you need.
