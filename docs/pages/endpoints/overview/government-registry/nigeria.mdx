# 🇳🇬 Nigeria

Official government registry checks for Nigeria.

## API Reference

- [API Reference](/api)

## Endpoints

- [Nigeria BVN Verification](../ng-bvn-verification) — Verify Nigerian Bank Verification Numbers against the NIBSS database. ([API](/api))
- [Nigeria NIN Verification](../ng-nin-verification) — Verify Nigerian National Identification Numbers against the NIMC database. ([API](/api))
- [Nigeria Virtual NIN](../ng-virtual-nin-verification) — Verify Nigerian Virtual National Identification Numbers issued by NIMC. ([API](/api))
- [Nigeria Phone Verification](../ng-advanced-phone-number-verification) — Verify Nigerian phone numbers with advanced carrier and identity validation. ([API](/api))
- [Nigeria Phone Lookup](../ng-phone-number-lookup) — Lookup subscriber information associated with a Nigerian phone number. ([API](/api))
- [Nigeria CAC Lookup](../ng-cac-lookup) — Lookup company records from the Nigerian Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC). ([API](/api))
- [Nigeria Passport Verification](../ng-passport-verification) — Verify Nigerian international passport numbers against NIS records. ([API](/api))
