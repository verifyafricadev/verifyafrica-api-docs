# Compliance & Screening

Screen individuals and businesses against sanctions lists, PEP databases, adverse media, and corporate registries. AML, Business AML, and KYB all support **link** (hosted) and **direct** modes. KYB also has three bases: `search`, `document`, and `document_purchase`.

- [Business AML Screening](/api/single-compliance-screening#business-aml-screening)
- [KYB](/api/single-compliance-screening#kyb-know-your-business)
- [Supported countries](/api/single-identity-verification#supported-countries)

## Available Endpoints

- [AML Screening](../aml-screening) — Screen individuals against global sanctions lists, PEP databases, and adverse media sources. ([API](/api/single-compliance-screening#aml-screening))
- [Business AML Screening](../business-aml-screening) — Screen businesses and corporate entities against global sanctions lists and adverse media. ([API](/api/single-compliance-screening#business-aml-screening))
- [KYB - Know Your Business](../kyb-screening) — Search, document, and document-purchase Know Your Business checks. ([API](/api/single-compliance-screening#kyb-know-your-business))

## Getting Started

Use the product create endpoints:

- `POST https://api.verifyafrica.io/api/v2/public/verifications/requests/aml_screening/`
- `POST https://api.verifyafrica.io/api/v2/public/verifications/requests/business_aml_screening/`
- `POST https://api.verifyafrica.io/api/v2/public/verifications/requests/kyb/`

Retrieve results with `GET https://api.verifyafrica.io/api/v2/public/verifications/requests/?verification_id={verification_id}`.

Authenticate with `Authorization: Bearer <VA-…api-key>`. The product path selects the verification type — do not send `verification_type`. See each endpoint page for required `input_data` fields.
